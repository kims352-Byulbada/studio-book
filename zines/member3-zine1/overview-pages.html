EyeCandy is a visual design studio in New York. This autumn, each of our designers took a brand and gave it a fall season.

Then we made the thing you could hold, wear or keep: a record, a coffee collection, a condom pack, a receipt, a merch line. Every prototype comes with its own zine, so you can open it and see how it works.
