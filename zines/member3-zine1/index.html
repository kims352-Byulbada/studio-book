Why a fall edition?

The weather turns, the plans move indoors, and people pair up. A seasonal drop makes protection part of the mood instead of an afterthought.

Leaf-fold wrapper

The packet opens in two easy tears, shaped like a leaf and easy to find in the dark.

Season-matched colours

Three packs designed to sit on a nightstand, not hide at the bottom of a drawer.

Friendly voice

Short, warm copy on every pack. No shame, no awkwardness, just a nudge.

Limited run

A fall-only collection that returns with a new palette every year.
